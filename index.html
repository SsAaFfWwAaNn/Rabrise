Rabrise

Something is wrong with the animals in this forest. They are sick, feverish and foaming, and they do not stop. You have a pistol, a flashlight and the dark. Stay out of the water, keep moving, and stay alive until it ends.

MoveW A S D or arrow keys
Aim and shootMouse, hold left button
ReloadR (shotgun loads one shell at a time, shooting interrupts it)
WeaponsWalk over a gun crate to pick it up. It replaces the gun you carry.
TorchF turns the flashlight on and off
PauseP

Touch controls: left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires. Buttons on the right reload, toggle the torch and pause. Walk over a gun crate to pick it up. Landscape works best.
